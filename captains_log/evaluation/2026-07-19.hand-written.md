# Captain's Log — Sunday 2026-07-19

A new floor hire was walked through the POS, the curbside queue and the label
printer on their first Sunday, and the morning opened with the store remote
misbehaving again — the lights took four presses to respond and staff put it
down to the batteries, the same failure that killed the music-skip button added
to the remote earlier. The label printer also spent part of the day on the wrong
settings and was not printing labels correctly until it was fixed.

The day's open question came from yesterday: someone arrived to collect an order
on a friend's behalf, but that order had already been dissolved and the stock
returned to the warehouse, and nobody has gone back to the customer about it. A
paper-canister question also came in on a ticket, asking what the canister is
made of and whether it can be reused.

Saffron was asked for and the shop does not carry it — staff mentioned someone
locally is trying to grow it, which is the only route to stocking it. A customer
working through the tulsi range asked how Krishna, Vana and Rama differ and
bought Vana on the lemon note; another asked to sample the buchu-and-rooibos
blend and then asked for the more expensive version. Staff also flagged that the
sample cups have no rim to grip.

Closing did not balance. The drawer came up **$70 over**, and the working theory
is an order that appeared to decline and was re-rung on a second register: the
transaction details showed the first attempt failing, but the amount may have
gone through anyway, which would mean a customer was charged twice. It needs to
be checked against the card record tomorrow before anyone assumes it is a
counting error. Yesterday's drawer was off by seven.
