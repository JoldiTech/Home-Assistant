# Captain's Log — Tuesday 2026-07-21

Most of the morning went into training a new floor hire on the register — cash,
custom card entry, gift cards, voids and the batch close — with the shop in
matching tie-dye, which a customer noticed and asked about. The training doubled
as an audit: working through practice sales surfaced that greeting cards are
inconsistent everywhere. Most ring up at $6.50 in the system, the floor sign
says $5.50 unless marked, and the Red Cap and Bad Betty cards are actually $5.
One customer was told all of hers were $5.50 and rung up on the cheaper listing;
staff want the card listings consolidated rather than patched sale by sale.

The register itself misbehaved twice. A $5.30 card sale cancelled itself while
the customer was still at the PIN screen, which nobody had seen before. And the
refund control on a threshold transaction still does not work as expected: the
whole-transaction refund arrow stays dead until every line item has been marked
individually, which is only discoverable by trial. New phones are going in, and
at least one call today did not ring through to the shop at all.

Stock and orders left several threads open. Vietnam black tea is still out with
no restock date to give the regular who asks for it. The chamomile on the shelf
is Star West stock bought at full price rather than the shop's own, and the
better grade is at the warehouse waiting for someone to drive up. Two curbside
orders both need a tea the shop is short of. A package mailed on June 13 is
showing as refused or uncollected. A customer's enamel teapot arrived with the
enamel blown off the base and was replaced from the last one in stock. Off the
floor, a sponsorship request came in for a youth soccer team's 2026 season, and
a customer asked to be told when unsweetened Insta Masala Chai is back.
