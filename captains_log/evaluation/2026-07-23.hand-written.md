# Captain's Log — Thursday 2026-07-23

Rain, and everyone said so — it stayed overcast and in the seventies most of the
morning instead of the 95° that had been forecast, and staff put the strong
opening stretch down to the weather driving people in off the street. Traffic
held through the afternoon, weighted toward long browsing visits rather than
quick pickups.

Two supply notes surfaced at the counter. A roasted Japanese twig green tea can
no longer be sourced and staff are telling customers to buy it while the shelf
stock lasts. The matcha kits are from a new supplier after the previous one
became unavailable, and staff are asking customers who buy one to report back on
how the whisk's opening mechanism feels. A customer also asked after the
double-sided pot/spoon measuring scoop the shop used to carry, said she had
bought one several years ago, and asked for it to be brought back. Ruby
Refresher and barley both came up as things the shop had recently run out of.

Pickup went wrong once: an order placed thirty minutes earlier was not showing
on the shop's screen and the customer had to wait while it was refreshed into
view. Staff described this as standing behaviour — orders come in but do not
appear until the screen is refreshed several times — and named a related cause
for customers arriving with no notice: opting out of the newsletter also drops
them from the list that sends the "your order is ready" email. Separately,
someone came in on behalf of an early-September Japanese cultural festival
asking whether the shop would vend or run a tea demonstration there; the answer
was that the equipment is not the problem, staffing it is.

Closing was tight on change: the drawer was short of ones and fives, tips were
broken up to cover it, an IOU was left in the drawer, and a bank run went on
tomorrow's list.
