# Captain's Log — Sunday 2026-07-26

The phones stopped passing calls through to the warehouse mid-morning — a test
call from a cell rang out without transferring — so anything that came in for
the warehouse today may simply not have reached anyone. The floor itself was
quiet early ("everybody must be at church") and then took a rush off the
farmers' market before settling into a steady afternoon of browsing visits.

Two stock-data problems surfaced at the register. Buchu still has no entry in
the system and is being rung up as Earl Grey because the price matches, so both
products' sales figures are wrong every time someone buys it. And Grand Keemun
is still priced at its pre-tariff cost — a customer bought eight ounces at the
old price today — because the price rise was never entered.

Storage is the other running theme. The fridge was found wedged open by a red
basket and is close to full; adagio and metro matcha have started appearing in
it alongside the sugimoto without anyone knowing whether that is a deliberate
change, and heavy stock like insta-chai is being shelved high enough that
getting it down is not safe. On the floor, chai is split across three different
shelves while honey sits consolidated, which is how a large bag of masala chai
went missing today; the suggestion on the floor was to consolidate the chai and
give Earl Grey its own shelf. Two cases of the traditional Sav-on 5oz gift tins
— 144 of them — were ordered for downtown.

Product notes: the new batch of Witch's Broom Puerh is visibly lighter and
tastes different from the last, and a regular sampled it before committing.
Cinnamon sticks came up twice and the shop only carries chips. A customer wanted
a fruit tisane with chamomile in it, which does not exist here and was offered
as a custom blend instead. A roasted yaupon sampled from a Texas supplier was
excellent, but that supplier never came back to us.
